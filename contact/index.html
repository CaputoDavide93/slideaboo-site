Common questions

How do I open the grown-ups’ settings?

Hold the faint gear button in the corner for two seconds; a ring fills while you hold it. A quick tap only shows a hint, so small hands rarely get in.

The puzzles are too hard, or too easy.

In the settings, choose your child’s age under Where puzzles start. After that the puzzles adjust by themselves: three in a row solved without a hint make the next a little harder, and one that needed two or more hints makes the next easier.

How do I change what I chose in the first setup?

Everything from the setup is in the grown-ups’ settings: age, language and voice, the name, sounds and play time. Start again at the bottom erases the name and offers the setup again next time.

Can I turn off the monkey or the numbers?

Yes. Cheeky monkey and Number puzzles are switches in the settings. Bigger puzzles (4×3 and 4×4) is off until you switch it on.

A screen says it’s time for a break, or for a book.

After about 30 minutes of play, Slideaboo suggests a break between puzzles; if you set a bedtime, it suggests a book from an hour before it. To play on, hold the button in the corner for two seconds. Both are in the settings under Play time.

Why does it make sounds when my iPhone is on silent?

The app plays its sounds like a game or music app, so they play with the ringer switch on silent. Use the volume buttons to turn them down.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Every puzzle and setting is free, with no purchases, subscriptions or ads.

I have a new phone.

Install Slideaboo again. The only things it keeps are the settings, so choose the age and the switches again if they didn’t come across.
