Why Slideaboo exists

Why it exists

Small children love putting a picture back together by themselves. Many puzzle apps for them add stars, timers and locked pictures, or stop after a few puzzles to ask for money.

Slideaboo takes one picture apart slowly and lets your child slide it back, with you beside them. The puzzles get a little harder after a few easy ones and easier after a hard one. It leaves out scores, timers, ads and anything to buy.

Why use it

Grows one step at a time

From a two-piece swap to a 3×3 square, each new puzzle changes one small thing.

Help, never a score

Peek and the hint lightbulb are always there. No stars, scores or timers.

Nothing to buy

Every puzzle and setting is here from the start. No ads, no purchases, no subscription.

It stays on this device

No account, internet connection or tracking. Only these settings, and a name if you add one, are saved, and you choose them.

A calm puzzle, played together.
