# ❓ FAQ

## What is this repository?

It is the public website for the Slideaboo app: a home page, the privacy policy, a contact and support page and a philosophy page, each in English and Italian. GitHub Pages serves it.

## Does the site use cookies, analytics or scripts?

No. The pages are static HTML and CSS, and `make check` fails if a script, form or iframe appears. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs, which the privacy policy says.

## Where is the privacy policy?

At <https://caputodavide93.github.io/slideaboo-site/privacy/>, and in Italian at <https://caputodavide93.github.io/slideaboo-site/it/privacy/>. The app's settings and the store listings link to it.

## Does the app collect my child's data?

The policy says it does not: no account, no internet connection, no analytics, ads or tracking. It keeps the grown-ups' settings on the device, and a first name only if you add one. Read the [policy](https://caputodavide93.github.io/slideaboo-site/privacy/) for the exact wording and its effective date. What the shipped app does beyond what the policy states is unverified here.

## Is the app free?

Yes, according to the policy and the contact page: no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

## Which languages are there?

English and Italian. The language switch in each page header links to the other version.

## Can I use the pages for my own app?

The code is MIT licensed, so the layout and styles are free to reuse. The text is the Slideaboo policy and describes that app only; write your own statements and check them against your own app.

## How do I report a problem with a page?

Open an issue with the page address, your browser and operating system, and a screenshot for layout problems. [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) lists what to include. A security problem goes through a private advisory, as [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) explains.

## Why is the support address not in the README?

The README and docs do not repeat it so the address lives in one place, on the contact page, where the app and the stores point.
