Privacy policy

Slideaboo doesn’t collect any data about you or your child. The little it keeps stays on your device.

Effective 4 October 2026. Applies to the Slideaboo apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and to this website.

In short

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No internet. The app never connects to the internet. On Android it doesn’t even have permission to.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No third-party code for any of these.
  • Nothing to buy. No purchases, subscriptions or ads, so no payment details either.
  • No AI. The puzzles follow fixed rules that run on your device.

What the app keeps, and where

Only the grown-ups’ settings: the child’s age you chose (as a range, such as “3 to 4 years”), and whether number puzzles, bigger puzzles and the monkey are on. They are kept in the app’s own preferences on your device, so the game opens the way you left it. The developer never receives them.

How your child is doing (which puzzles, how hard, how many hints) is held only while the app is open, to choose the next puzzle, and is gone when it closes. The app keeps no name, photo, recording, birthday or anything typed.

On Android, the app turns off Google backup and device-to-device transfer for its settings, so on a new phone you choose them again. On iPhone and iPad, the settings can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, like other apps’ preferences; that backup is yours and never reaches the developer.

Permissions

None. The app never asks for your microphone, camera, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

Sounds and the voice

The sound effects and the narrator’s short praise (“Well done!”) were made in advance with ElevenLabs and come inside the app. Nothing is recorded, generated or downloaded while you play.

Links

The grown-ups’ settings have two links: this privacy policy and the contact page. They open in your browser only after a grown-up answers a sum, and the app never loads them itself.

Payments

None. There is nothing to buy in Slideaboo, and no library in it sends data to anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its settings from your device.

Children

Slideaboo is made for young children to play with a grown-up nearby. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and aren’t shared with anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Anything that would mean data leaving your device would be explained in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: see the contact page. Slideaboo is made by Davide Caputo.
