Slide the tiles, and the picture is whole again.

Slideaboo is a calm sliding-picture puzzle for small children and the grown-up beside them. A picture comes apart slowly, and your child slides it back together. On iPhone, iPad and Android.

  • It starts with a two-piece swap and grows, one small step at a time, to a 3×3 square, with 4×3 and 4×4 for older children if you switch them on. Three puzzles in a row without a hint make the next one a little harder; one that needed hints makes it easier. No levels on screen.
  • Peek shows the whole picture while it is held, and the helping hand lights a tile that moves the right way. They are always there, and nothing keeps score.
  • Every third puzzle can have numbered tiles, with dots to count on the easier ones. On the hardest puzzles, a cheeky monkey sometimes slides a few tiles away.
  • When the picture is whole, a chime and a short “Well done!” from the narrator. Nothing plays on by itself: the next puzzle comes when you tap.
  • Free and complete, with nothing to buy: no purchases, no subscription, no ads.
  • Nothing leaves your device. No account, no internet connection, no analytics, no tracking.
